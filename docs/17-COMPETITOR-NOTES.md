# 17 — Product validation notes

The product is informed by broad targeted research of existing Minecraft tooling.

## Almost Unified

Validates that cross-mod material unification is a real pack-author problem and a substantial category. Material Nexus should differentiate through its visual recipe-family analysis, explainability and explicit policy workflow rather than attempting to be a larger generic unifier.

## Recipe Generator

Validates demand for GUI-driven recipe editing. Material Nexus narrows this to material-aware families and pack-wide consequences.

## JEI / EMI

These are essential presentation ecosystems. Material Nexus should integrate rather than compete with them.

## Design consequence

Do not become a generic scripting platform, logistics mod or persistent server database. The strongest product is a focused material/recipe control plane for modpacks.
